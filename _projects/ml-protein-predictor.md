A transformer-based deep learning model for predicting protein secondary structure from amino acid sequences.

## Approach

- Leverages attention mechanisms to capture long-range dependencies in protein sequences
- Trained on curated PDB datasets with 3-state and 8-state classification
- Achieves competitive accuracy with state-of-the-art methods

## Tech Stack

- PyTorch for model development
- Weights & Biases for experiment tracking
- FastAPI for model serving
